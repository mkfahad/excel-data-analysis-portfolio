# 📊 Global Superstore Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes the **Global Superstore Sales Dataset** to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product trends, and regional performance. The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel** with Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, Slicers, and an Interactive Dashboard.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories.
* Evaluate regional and market-wise sales trends.
* Monitor order quantity and customer purchasing patterns.
* Discover profitable products and segments.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains transactional sales data with details such as:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer Information
* Segment
* Region & Market
* Product Category & Sub-Category
* Sales
* Quantity
* Profit
* Shipping Cost
* Order Priority

### Dataset Size

* **50,000+ records**
* Multiple regions and global markets
* Multiple product categories and sub-categories

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

* Total sales generated across all regions.
* Year-wise and month-wise sales trend analysis.
* Identification of peak sales periods.

### 2. Profitability Analysis

* Overall profit generated.
* Most profitable categories and sub-categories.
* Loss-making products and regions.

### 3. Regional Analysis

* Sales by Region.
* Profit by Region.
* Regional contribution to overall revenue.

### 4. Product Analysis

* Top-selling categories.
* Top-selling sub-categories.
* Quantity sold by product category.

### 5. Customer Segment Analysis

* Consumer Segment
* Corporate Segment
* Home Office Segment

Comparison of sales and profitability across customer segments.

## Key Insights

* Certain regions contribute significantly to overall sales and profit.
* A small number of product categories generate the majority of revenue.
* Some high-sales products produce low profit margins.
* Seasonal trends can be observed in monthly sales performance.
* Customer segments show different purchasing behaviors and profitability levels.
